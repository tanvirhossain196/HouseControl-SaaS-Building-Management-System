# Logo

Put your logo here as `house-control.jpg` and it replaces the built-in mark
everywhere — the marketing header, the app header, and the auth screens.

- **File name:** `house-control.jpg` exactly
- **Size:** square, 256×256 or larger. It is displayed at 32×32.
- **Format:** `.jpg` as named. For a PNG or SVG, change `LOGO_SRC` in
  `src/components/layout/logo.tsx`.

With no file here the app draws its own mark instead. Nothing breaks and
nothing needs configuring — the fallback runs on the image's `onError`.
